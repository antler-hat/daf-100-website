Privacy Policy for DAF-100

Effective date: August 15, 2026

DAF-100 ("DAF-100") is designed to run locally on your device.

Data Collection

DAF-100 does not collect personal information.

DAF-100 does not collect usage analytics, diagnostics, or crash reports.

DAF-100 does not use advertising SDKs or tracking technologies.

Data Use

Because DAF-100 does not collect personal information, it does not use personal information for any purpose.

Data Sharing

DAF-100 does not share personal information with third parties.

Local Device Storage

DAF-100 stores your project/session data locally on your device (for example, autosave and saved projects) so core features work as expected.

This data is not transmitted to developer-owned servers.

Accounts and Sign-In

DAF-100 does not require account creation or user sign-in.

Purchases

DAF-100 offers optional consumable in-app tip purchases processed by Apple through the App Store.

DAF-100 does not require purchases to access core functionality and does not include paid subscriptions.

DAF-100 uses the purchase result only to complete the transaction and does not store it or send it to developer-owned servers.

Apple may process payment information and purchase history according to the Apple Privacy Policy.

DAF-100 does not receive your payment card or bank account details.

Children’s Privacy

DAF-100 is not directed to children under 13, but it can be used by a general audience.

DAF-100 does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

DAF-100 does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Security

Since data is stored locally, the security of your data depends in part on your device and operating system protections.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this policy changes, the "Effective date" above will be updated.
